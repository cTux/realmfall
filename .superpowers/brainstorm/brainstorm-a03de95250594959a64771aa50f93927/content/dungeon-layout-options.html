Which dungeon shape should be the baseline?

Each option assumes one themed dungeon instance, at least 200 hexes, walls as impassable terrain, and one chest placed near the end.

A. Branching Spine

One readable main route with side branches for fights and detours. The chest sits at the far sanctum, so the player always feels a clear push toward an endpoint.

B. Rooms And Corridors

Distinct chambers linked by narrow routes. This gives good combat pockets, clear places for themed terrain clusters, and a natural final room for a chest or boss-like encounter.

C. Dense Maze

A labyrinth-first dungeon with more wrong turns and longer navigation. It leans hardest into exploration pressure, but it risks feeling slower if movement pacing is not tuned carefully.
